Public reference

Data Dictionary

Plain-language definitions for the constrained fields and states used by the public monitoring experience. Definitions describe presentation meaning, not financial significance.

Before reading

Research / monitoring only

Fields are not trading signals and provide no buy/sell/hold recommendation. Human review is required.

Core concepts

Observation, candidate, status, and outcome

Research observation
A constrained record or aggregate retained for review. It does not predict what happens next.
Candidate
A monitoring item that met a system classification for research attention. It is not an approval or action.
Status
A textual label describing availability, processing, monitoring, or reviewed outcome context.
Outcome
An explicitly defined observed classification. It does not mean profit, success, or recommendation.

Artifact context

Availability and freshness fields

generated_at_utc / generated_at
Artifact-provided generation timestamp. It does not independently prove source freshness.
available
Whether a summary reports itself as available. It is not a quality or completeness guarantee.
files
Manifest structure containing approved public-file metadata such as filename, existence, modification timestamp, purpose, size, and type.
mode / scope
Operating-boundary labels. They do not describe market direction.
financial_advice / live_trading
Boundary flags. The public experience must not reinterpret them as capabilities.

Confidence language

Confidence is bounded context

A confidence or strength band, when present, is a system classification. It is not probability of profit, certainty, suitability, or a reason to act.

State definitions

Missing, stale, unavailable, and insufficient data

Missing
An expected value is absent. Do not substitute a positive or negative meaning.
Stale
Information is older than a separately approved threshold. Show the timestamp and basis for the label.
Unavailable
The approved source cannot provide the information. Do not present cached information as current without labeling.
Insufficient history
The observation window does not support the classification. It is not a negative result.
Empty
The artifact loaded but contains no displayable records.
Failure
The interface could not read or present the artifact. No conclusion should be drawn.

Interpretation boundary

Definitions do not create authority.

Public AI output is disabled unless separately authorized. Publication is disabled unless separately authorized. See the Limitations page before using any field for research review.
