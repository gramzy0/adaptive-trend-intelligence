Explicit boundaries

What this demo does not do

The demo is intentionally limited. Its public output supports inspection, not action.

No financial advice

Nothing presented is individualized guidance or a basis for financial action.

No trading signals

Statuses and observations are monitoring context, not directions to trade.

No buy/sell/hold recommendation

No label or output recommends a transaction or position.

No automated execution

The static site cannot place orders or initiate an action workflow.

No private/internal data exposure

Only sanitized public artifacts belong on this public surface.

Human review required

A person must check freshness, definitions, limitations, and context before drawing a conclusion.

What is this?

A constrained public view of monitoring artifacts and system-level status.

02

What can I inspect?

Snapshot metrics, CSV research artifacts, field definitions, and explicit limitations.

03

How fresh is it?

Read the generated timestamp, then treat freshness as unconfirmed unless source recency is independently established.

04

What should I not infer?

No prediction, recommendation, certainty, suitability, or permission to act follows from a public record.

05

Where next?

Open the dashboard, then use the dictionary and limitations before interpreting any field.
