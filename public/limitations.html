Interpretation boundary

What the public snapshot cannot establish.

A clear boundary is part of the product. Read these limits before using any timestamp, status, count, candidate, or history record.

Non-negotiable

Research / monitoring only

This site is not trading signals and provides no financial advice, no buy/sell/hold recommendation, and no automated execution. Human review is required.

01

No predictive certainty

No public record claims or establishes future direction, probability, expected return, repeatability, or predictive certainty.

02

No guaranteed freshness

A generated timestamp is metadata. Source observations can be older, delayed, revised, or unavailable; freshness is never guaranteed.

03

Stale or incomplete artifacts

The public snapshot can be stale or incomplete. Missing fields and unavailable files support no favorable or unfavorable conclusion.

04

Limited public surface

Public data is a constrained subset, not the full internal system. It omits private sources, internal context, logic, controls, and review records.

05

No action workflow

The site provides no brokerage, portfolio management, allocation, entry, exit, order, deployment, or automated trading execution capability.

06

Human review remains required

Automated labels are inputs to investigation, not approvals. Independent analysis must check context, definitions, recency, and uncertainty.

Do not infer

Status is not direction.

  • A candidate is not an opportunity.
  • A score count is not an investment score.
  • An exposure mode is not a market regime.
  • An available artifact is not necessarily current or correct.
  • Historical outcomes do not establish future results.
  • Absence does not mean safety, insignificance, or zero risk.

If something is unclear

Stop at uncertainty.

Do not fill gaps with assumptions. Check the Data Dictionary, compare the generated timestamp with an approved freshness standard, establish semantic source freshness separately, and seek appropriate independent expertise.
