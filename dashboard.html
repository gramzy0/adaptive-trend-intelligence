Candidate interpretation

A candidate is a monitoring item—not an instruction.

Research observation

Candidate fields group public observations by asset type, benchmark, watch tier, signal-strength band, market-cap band, current state, weekly state, observation bucket, and status note. The table below explains presentation treatment without displaying or ranking artifact values.

Status language

Data states stay visible and textual.

Empty

No displayable records

The artifact loaded but contains no rows suitable for this view.

Missing

Expected field absent

No substitute meaning is assigned.

Stale

Timestamp needs review

Do not treat older information as current.

Insufficient history

Observation window too short

No outcome classification is supported.

Unavailable

Artifact cannot be read

Absence does not support a conclusion.

Failure

Presentation could not complete

Retry later and review the limitations.

Research-only boundary

Observation requires judgment.
