Limitations and safety boundaries

The public experience is intentionally constrained. Its records support research review; they do not establish what anyone should do.

Principal disclaimer

Research / monitoring only.

Content is not financial advice, not trading signals, and provides no buy/sell/hold recommendation. Human review is required before interpretation or any downstream decision.

01

Data dependency

Outputs depend on approved source data and constrained public artifacts. Data may be delayed, incomplete, stale, unavailable, incorrect, or revised.

02

No completeness guarantee

Public outputs intentionally omit private sources, internal logic, review context, and non-public fields. Absence does not prove that no relevant information exists.

03

No accuracy or timeliness guarantee

An artifact timestamp reports generation context only. It does not establish that every source observation is current or correct.

04

No execution workflow

The public site provides no trading, brokerage, portfolio-management, allocation, entry, exit, or execution function.

05

Past observations are limited

Historical records and outcome labels do not establish future performance, expected return, repeatability, or suitability.

06

Human review remains necessary

Automated classifications are inputs to investigation, not approvals. Independent analysis and suitable controls remain necessary.

Data-state limits

Absence and delay have distinct meanings.

Empty
The artifact loaded with no displayable records; it does not mean no risk or no noteworthy condition.
Missing
A field is absent; it must not be replaced with an inferred value.
Stale
Information may be older than an approved threshold; review its timestamp before interpretation.
Unavailable or failure
The artifact or presentation cannot be read; no conclusion follows.
Insufficient history
The available window cannot support the classification; it is not evidence for or against an outcome.

Coverage boundary

Constrained and reviewed

Public presence does not authorize broader internal data, functionality, methodology claims, or system capabilities. Coverage labels describe monitoring context only.

Label interpretation

Status is not direction.

Candidate, monitoring, confidence, state, and outcome labels describe bounded system classifications. They do not predict price direction, rank financial opportunities, demonstrate an edge, or indicate suitability. Color is never sufficient to convey status; read the text label and definition.

Controlled actions

Public display is not publication authority.

Public AI output remains disabled unless separately authorized. Publication remains a separately controlled action and is disabled unless separately authorized. Deployment, synchronization, hosting, upload, and release require explicit human decisions and execution authorization.

Full interpretation boundary

No public record is a recommendation.

Nothing on this site constitutes financial, investment, legal, tax, or other professional advice. Nothing is a solicitation, endorsement, guarantee, or assessment of fitness for purpose. Any real-world use requires independent review and appropriate professional guidance.
